# clarify

Notes like these: "I don't get it", "what does that mean", "the wording", "say it simpler", "the narration is off".
The words: narration, captions, titles and labels.

## Assess
- Read every line aloud at the speed it plays. Mark each one the audience in the brief wouldn't understand on first
  hearing, and each name the voice says that isn't on screen when it's said.
- Find jargon, abstractions and lines that describe what the picture already shows.

## Change
- Plain, specific words in the person's own vocabulary. One idea a line.
- Narration says why it matters; the picture shows what it is.
- Every name said is shown; every thing the viewer has to find is named.
- Re-time cues to the new words from the transcript, never by eye.

## Verify
- Play it with sound only, then with picture only. Both make sense; together they don't repeat each other.
