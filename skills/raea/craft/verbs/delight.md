# delight

Notes like these: "it's fine but forgettable", "add something fun", "a little surprise", "make me smile". One small
detail that rewards attention, in the piece's own voice.

## Assess
- Is the piece right first? Delight on a piece that doesn't land is noise: fix the note's real problem before this.
- Find a moment with room: a hold, a transition, the end card, a game's idle or its win.

## Change
- One detail, maybe two: a character's blink, a sound on a hit, the logo doing one thing it only does here, a line
  of copy that's a little warmer than it has to be.
- From the brand and the subject, never generic (no confetti unless confetti is the point).
- Small and quick: it shouldn't take the eye from what the moment is about.

## Verify
- Watch it as someone seeing it for the first time: the detail is noticed second, not first.
- Take it out and watch again: if nothing is lost, it wasn't worth keeping.
