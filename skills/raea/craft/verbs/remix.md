# remix

Notes like these: "the music's too loud", "can't hear her", "needs a sound there", "the mix", "it sounds cheap". The
sound: voice, music, foley and the master.

## Assess
- Listen once for the voice alone: is every word clear over everything else?
- Then listen for what's filling space rather than marking moments: a bed at one level under everything, a whoosh on
  every entrance.
- With a song, check the cuts and hits against its map.

## Change
- The voice first; duck music and foley under it.
- Sound marks moments: a hit on the cut that matters, silence before the drop. Remove what only fills.
- Music: a library instrument first; TokenSynth takes are curated, not trusted. Under a voice, a sparse score.
- Master at -14 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below -1 dBTP.

## Verify
- Measure the loudness. Listen through once on small speakers and once on headphones.
