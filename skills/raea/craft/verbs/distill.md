# distill

Notes like these: "too long", "too much going on", "what's the point", "simplify", "cut it down". Fewer ideas, lines
and parts, until what's left is the piece.

## Assess
- Write the brief's `believe:` line next to the piece. Mark every scene, line, layer and sound that doesn't serve it.
- Find the repeats: two shots that say the same thing, two lines that make the same point, two sounds in one moment.

## Change
- Cut whole things before trimming parts: a scene, a line, a layer. Expect to cut good things.
- Merge what repeats into the stronger one.
- Re-time what's left so it breathes. Distilling isn't only cutting; the time you free goes to the moments that matter.

## Verify
- Say the piece in one sentence after watching it. It matches `believe:`.
- The length is within what was asked.
