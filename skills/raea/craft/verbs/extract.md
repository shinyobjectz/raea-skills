# extract

Notes like these: "use the same look as last time", "make a brand kit from this", "we don't have guidelines, use
the video". A brand kit read from finished work rather than a website.

## Assess
- Gather the finished pieces the person names: renders in `out/`, earlier projects, their files in
  `raea library search`.
- From stills across them, find what repeats: the two or three colours, the faces and weights of type, the logo and
  how it's shown, how things move and cut.

## Change
- With a site, start from `raea brand DOMAIN`, then add what the work shows that the site doesn't.
- Write what you found into the kit: `brand/brand.css` (`--brand-*` colours and fonts), `brand/brand.json`, and
  BRAND.md (what each colour means, the motion and cutting habits) (`raea guide motion/brand`).
- Take logos from the source files the person gives, never from a frame grab.
- Mark guesses as guesses in BRAND.md, and ask the person to confirm them.

## Verify
- A test card in the new kit sits beside a still of the old work and reads as the same brand.
