# recut

Notes like these: "the order is wrong", "start with that", "that cut is jarring", "swap those", "the ending". The order
of scenes and how shots join.

## Assess
- Does each scene depend only on what came before it? Does the strongest image come at the right place: the hook
  first in a pitch, the payoff last in an explain, the poster frame where the story builds to?
- At each cut, check what joins the two shots: motion matched, a planned reversal, or a hard cut on a hit. A cut with
  no reason is a jolt.

## Change
- Move whole scenes, then re-time cues to the transcript.
- Join motion to motion: a shot ends still moving and the next starts moving the same way.
- Cut on the song's beats and events, never at a constant tempo.

## Verify
- Watch every changed cut at full speed, then the whole piece. Nothing jolts that shouldn't.
