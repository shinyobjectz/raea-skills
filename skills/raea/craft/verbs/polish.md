# polish

The last pass before the person sees it: works through the latest critique, in its own direction. It never hides a
redesign; if the direction itself is wrong, say so and go back to shaping.

## How
- Open the latest critique. Work down from P0 to P3; each fix is the verb its issue calls for (quieter, retime,
  clarify, regrade, remix…).
- Then the floor, line by line, on the last render.
- Keep the strongest moment the critique named.

## Verify
- Render, look at the frames and open the whole export. Mark each critique issue fixed or say why not.
