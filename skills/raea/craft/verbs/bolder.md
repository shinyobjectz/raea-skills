# bolder

Notes like these: "more punch", "it's flat", "make it hit", "bigger", "it's safe". More conviction where a beat already
exists. Almost always one beat, not the whole piece.

## Assess
- Find the beat the note means: the hook, the reveal, the drop, the payoff. What should hit there and doesn't?
- Is it weak because of what's on screen, or because of what's around it? A beat often feels flat because the moment
  before it is just as loud.

## Change
- Contrast first: make the moment before quieter or stiller, so the beat lands by difference.
- Then the beat itself: a closer frame, a harder cut on the downbeat, a larger scale change, one strong sound under it.
- Commit to one move. Two big moves at once cancel each other out.
- Don't add effects to make it bold. Bold is a decision, not a filter.

## Verify
- Watch the beat with the two seconds before it. It hits now, and nothing else got louder to compete.
