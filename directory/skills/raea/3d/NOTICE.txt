raea's Bevy skills library (sdk/guides/craft/bevy.md and bevy/*.md, copied into the raea skill's 3D topic files in raea-skills (skills/skills/raea/3d/)) is written for
raea/3d. No text was copied from other packs. Its layout (a short router, one file per topic, each with a
checked example and a list of common mistakes) follows EnzeD/r3f-skills (no license stated; cited, not copied),
chrisgliddon/bevy-skills (MIT) and gamedev-skills' bevy-ecs (Apache-2.0). Bevy API facts come from Bevy's own v0.19.1
examples and migration guides (MIT OR Apache-2.0). The survey with links is in sdk/raea/docs/bevy-coverage.md.

The Solari and water topics (bevy/solari.md, bevy/water.md) cite, and don't copy: Bevy's bevy_solari docs, its
examples/3d/solari.rs and its implementation (v0.20, MIT OR Apache-2.0); JMS55's "Realtime Raytracing in Bevy 0.17
(Solari)"; GPU Gems 1 ch. 1, "Effective Water Simulation from Physical Models"; Tessendorf's "Simulating Ocean
Water"; an ocean simulation and rendering survey; Neopallium/bevy_water and mate-h/bevy_fft; and Bevy Elements'
water research note.
